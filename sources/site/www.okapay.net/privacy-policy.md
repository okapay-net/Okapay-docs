By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

When payments are processed through Okapay or through payment partners connected to Okapay, we may process information relating to transactions, including:

- Transaction amount

- Pages viewed

- API activity
- Error logs
- Security and fraud signals

If you contact us, we may retain:

- Your name
- Contact details
- Message content
- Attachments
- Support history
- Sales communications
- Compliance communications
