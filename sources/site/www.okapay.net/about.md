## What Okapay does

A technology layer that connects businesses with payment infrastructure and financial partners through one platform.

- Card payment processing
- Local and alternative payment methods
- Global payment acceptance
- Merchant onboarding
- Payment APIs
- Hosted and embedded checkout
- Merchant dashboards
- Transaction management
- Settlement and payout workflows
- Payment routing
- Risk and compliance workflows
- Platform and marketplace models

## Built for global commerce

Payment infrastructure is often fragmented by country, currency, method, and institution. Okapay is built to simplify that complexity through partnerships with processors, banks, and payment providers.

One platform. Multiple payment routes — international cards, Pix in Brazil, and regional methods across Asia — without forcing merchants to manage disconnected integrations.

Technology, merchant experience, and payment orchestration.

## Our vision

A global financial infrastructure layer so businesses can access payment capabilities without navigating a fragmented network of providers on their own.

Accept. Manage. Route. And move money globally.

## Our mission

Make global payments simpler, more accessible, and easier to integrate — so payment infrastructure is never the barrier to growing beyond borders.

## Responsible payment infrastructure

Trust, security, merchant verification, monitoring, fraud prevention, and compliance are fundamental. Where regulated payment services are required, Okapay works with financial institutions, processors, and acquiring partners rather than acting as a substitute for them.
